Apple · Machine Learning Research

Tianrong Chen.

Research Scientist / Cupertino, California

I build generative models and study how to make them more capable and more efficient.

My research spans large-scale language model pre-training, looped Transformers, and diffusion and flow-based models for language, images, and video. I am particularly interested in the connection between model architecture, computation, and learning.

I joined Apple in 2024 after completing my Ph.D. at Georgia Tech, advised by Evangelos A. Theodorou.
