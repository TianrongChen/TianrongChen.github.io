Apple · Machine Learning Research

Tianrong Chen.

Research Scientist / Cupertino, California

I build generative models and study how to make them more capable and more efficient.

My research spans language model pre-training, looped Transformers, and diffusion and flow-based models for language, images, video, and multimodal generation. I am particularly interested in the co-design of algorithms and infrastructure.

Data + infrastructure + co-designed algorithms = better models.

I joined Apple in 2024 after completing my Ph.D. at Georgia Tech, advised by Evangelos A. Theodorou.
